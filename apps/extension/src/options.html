IFP Subsidy Assistant

Saved.

What this extension does

  • Fills the IFP application form with data you already prepared in the platform.
  • Marks every box it touched, and lists every box it could not fill.
  • Runs only when you click, and only on the IFP portal.

What it never does

  • Press submit, or trigger a submit any other way.
  • Read, solve, forward or bypass a CAPTCHA.
  • Read a password field, or store your IFP credentials or cookies.
  • Run in the background, on a schedule, or on any other website.
  • Send the values it typed back to the platform — only field names and outcomes.
